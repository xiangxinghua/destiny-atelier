CRYSTALS FOR YOUR MBTI

5 Crystals Every INFP Needs (From an INFP Who's Tested Them)

May 2, 2026 · 7 min read · By Kai

If you're an INFP, you already know: you feel everything 10x more than people around you, you crash 10x harder, you live half your life inside your own head, and "self-care" advice from non-INFPs makes you want to scream. I'm an INFP. I've tested 30+ crystals over 4 years. These 5 are the ones that address the specific INFP failure modes — not generic "good vibes."

The 4 INFP struggles these crystals actually address

Every "crystals for INFP" article online is just a generic crystal list with the word INFP slapped on. I want this one to be different. Here are the specific INFP problems I'm targeting:

  1. Emotional flooding — you feel a feeling about a feeling about a feeling and end up in bed for 4 hours
  2. Perfection paralysis — the project HAS to be perfect or you can't release it, so it never gets released
  3. The void — those days where life feels meaningless and you're just going through motions
  4. Caring-too-much burnout — you absorb everyone's emotions and have nothing left for yourself

Each crystal below maps to one or more of these. Specific. Tested. INFP-approved.

The 5 crystals (in order I'd recommend buying them)

CRYSTAL 01

Amethyst — for emotional flooding

Of course amethyst is on the list. But here's the INFP-specific use: when you're spiraling because something small triggered something bigger, and you can already feel the next 4 hours being lost to the spiral.

What works: hold amethyst on your chest. Don't try to "process." Just feel the weight. Name what you're feeling out loud (you can whisper). "I am feeling rejected. I am feeling small. I am feeling 12 years old." Do this until the feeling has a name. Once it has a name, it's not endless anymore.

I went from 4-hour spirals to 30-minute spirals doing this consistently. Not zero. But manageable.

INFP RITUAL When the spiral starts: amethyst on chest, name the feeling out loud, set timer for 20 minutes. After 20 minutes, get up and drink water. Even if not "done," get up.
CRYSTAL 02

Citrine — for perfection paralysis

INFPs have this thing where the work has to fully express the inner vision or it's "fake." So we don't release it. So nothing gets out into the world. So we feel both stuck and unseen.

Citrine — the warm yellow "manifestation" stone — has been my anti-perfectionism tool. The trick is the framing: I tell myself the citrine is "for releasing the work, not perfecting it." Hold it before publishing/sharing/sending. Set the intention: "this version is good enough for today. The next version can be better."

Half the things I've shipped this year (including this site) wouldn't exist without this practice. Real citrine, FYI — most "citrine" sold cheaply is heat-treated amethyst. Doesn't matter that much, but real citrine is rarer.

INFP RITUAL Before publishing/sending anything: hold citrine. Say "this is good enough for today." Press send within 60 seconds.
CRYSTAL 03

Labradorite — for "the void" days

Every INFP knows "the void." Days where life feels meaningless. Where the things you usually love feel hollow. Where your inner life — usually your refuge — has gone grey.

Labradorite is the crystal I reach for on those days. Dark grey-green base with electric blue/gold flashes that appear when you tilt it. The literal magic of seeing color emerge from grey is the metaphor I need on void days. There IS color in the grey. I'm just not at the right angle right now.

Hold it, tilt slowly, watch for the flash. Do this for 5 minutes. The void doesn't always lift, but I'm reminded the void isn't permanent. That's enough to get through the day.

INFP RITUAL Void days: 5 minutes with labradorite by a window with good light. Tilt slowly. Catch the flash. Trust that color is still there, even if you can't see it from your current angle.
CRYSTAL 04

Black Tourmaline — for absorbing-too-much burnout

INFPs absorb other people's emotions like sponges. After a hard day with friends/family/clients, you come home and your body is heavy with feelings that aren't even yours.

Black tourmaline is the boundary stone. The protection isn't from "negative energy" in some woo sense — it's from your tendency to take on what isn't yours. I keep one in my purse for social situations. When I notice I'm starting to spiral about someone else's problem, I touch it as a reminder: "this is not your feeling to carry."

For severe empath burnout: black tourmaline on the bedside table, plus a 30-minute "decompression" ritual (no phone, no input, just being) before bed. Has saved me from at least a dozen burnout cycles.

INFP RITUAL Before social events: touch black tourmaline, say "I will hold space for them; I will not carry it home." After events: 30 minutes of decompression before consuming any input.
CRYSTAL 05

Rose Quartz — for the inner critic that hates you

The hardest part of being INFP for me isn't the feelings or the people-stuff. It's the inner critic. The voice that says I'm not doing enough, not creating enough, not living up to my potential, not being who I "should" be.

Rose quartz is the self-compassion practice I most needed. The crystal isn't important — the practice is. Hold rose quartz on your chest. Speak to yourself the way you would speak to your closest friend if they were having your exact day. "It's okay that you didn't get to everything. You're tired. You'll try again tomorrow."

This is hard for INFPs because we're trained to be hard on ourselves. The crystal is permission to soften the way we'd let ourselves soften toward someone else.

INFP RITUAL Nightly: rose quartz on chest, 3 breaths. Then: speak to yourself out loud the way you'd speak to your closest friend. Even if it feels weird. Especially if it feels weird.

How to actually start (the realistic version)

INFPs love systems we don't follow. So I'm going to make this dead simple:

  1. Buy ONE crystal first. If you spiral the most, get amethyst. If you don't ship work, get citrine. If you have void days, get labradorite. If you're burned out from people, get black tourmaline. If you hate yourself, get rose quartz.
  2. Use it for 30 days for ONE specific situation. The ritual matters more than which crystal.
  3. Don't add a second crystal until you're using the first one consistently. Adding crystals to avoid using the first one is classic INFP avoidance behavior. I've done it. Don't.
  4. Track lightly. Even 1 sentence in a notebook: "Day 14, amethyst before bed, slept better." Patterns will emerge.

Things to avoid (INFP-specific traps)

  • Don't treat crystals as a substitute for therapy. If you're depressed, you need a therapist. Crystals are a complement, not a replacement.
  • Don't over-research before starting. INFPs will read 40 articles instead of buying one $5 stone. Start. Then learn.
  • Don't perform spirituality. Crystals on Instagram don't do anything for you. Crystals you actually hold during hard moments do.
  • Don't expect a "feeling." Most INFPs are imaginative enough to convince themselves they feel energy. Then they doubt it. Then they spiral. Just use the stone as an anchor for the practice and let the practice be the work.

One INFP to another

You feel too much. You think too much. You care too much. The world keeps telling you to feel less, think less, care less. Crystals — the way I use them — don't change any of that. They give me a 60-second moment to honor that I'm built this way and to choose how to use the intensity instead of being run by it.

That's it. That's the whole INFP crystal practice. Start with one. See what happens.
